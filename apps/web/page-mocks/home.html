FAQ

  • 01Is PING Network only an MCN?
    No. While our MCN operates as a dedicated vertical, PING works across platform programs, brand partnerships, publishing, live commerce, and creator-led ecosystems. The MCN is one part of a much larger infrastructure.
  • 02Do you work with creators directly?
    Yes. We work with creators across growth strategy, monetisation, platform enablement, content scaling, and brand opportunities. Our approach is long-term and partnership-led.
  • 03Do you only work with large creators?
    No. We work across growth stages — from emerging creators to established names — depending on the program or ecosystem we are building.
  • 04What makes PING different from a traditional influencer agency?
    We build systems, not just campaigns. Our work spans creator infrastructure, platform partnerships, publishing engines, and monetisation models — not one-off influencer activations.
