Model 01

Content ID for Selected YouTube Videos

Designed for broadcasters, production houses, music labels, sports rights owners and anyone managing a large or valuable content library.

Instead of linking your whole YouTube channel, we protect only the content that matters most: premium episodes, viral clips, film scenes, sports highlights, or back-catalogue assets that continue to generate value over time.

Using YouTube's Content ID system, these selected assets are registered and tracked. Whenever unauthorised uploads surface, they can be blocked, claimed or monetised per your requirements — letting you retain control and recover lost revenue.

  • Asset-level Content ID registration
  • Block, claim or monetise per asset
  • No change to existing channel ops
  • Ideal for libraries & rights owners

Model 02

Full Channel-Level Protection

Built for creators, digital publishers, and fast-moving content teams who actively upload content on their own channels. Your channel is securely linked to PING's certified YouTube CMS, enabling channel-level Content ID protection.

Every new video you publish is automatically protected against unauthorised reuploads from the moment it goes live — no manual asset submission required.

As your content scales, your copyright protection and monetisation safeguards scale with it — without slowing down your publishing process.
