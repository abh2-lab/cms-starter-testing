Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 1, 2026

At Mill-Times Mumbai, we are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparency in how we handle your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding that information when you visit our website or use our services.

1

Information We Collect

We collect information that you provide directly to us, as well as data gathered automatically when you interact with our platform. This includes:

  • Personal identifiers such as your name, email address, and contact details when you submit a story, sign up for our newsletter, or reach out through our contact form.
  • Content submissions including stories, photographs, audio recordings, video files, and any accompanying metadata you provide as part of your citizen journalism contributions.
  • Technical data such as your IP address, browser type and version, operating system, device information, referring URLs, and pages visited on our site.
  • Usage analytics including time spent on pages, scroll depth, click patterns, and navigation paths that help us understand how readers engage with our content.
  • Location data when voluntarily provided alongside a story submission, such as the neighbourhood or region relevant to your report.
2

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • To review, edit, and publish story submissions in accordance with our editorial standards and guidelines.
  • To attribute published stories to their authors and maintain accurate bylines as part of our commitment to journalistic integrity.
  • To communicate with you about your submissions, editorial feedback, and updates to our platform or policies.
  • To deliver our Weekly Dispatch newsletter and other communications you have opted in to receive.
  • To improve our website, content curation, and user experience through aggregated analytics and usage patterns.
  • To detect and prevent fraud, spam, and security threats, and to ensure the safety of our community.

We will never sell your personal information to third parties. Your data is used solely to operate and improve the Mill-Times Mumbai platform and community.

3

Cookies and Tracking

Our website uses cookies and similar tracking technologies to enhance your browsing experience and gather analytical data. These include:

  • Essential cookies that are necessary for the website to function properly, such as session management and security features.
  • Analytics cookies that help us understand how visitors interact with our site. We use privacy-respecting analytics to track page views, session duration, and content engagement without building invasive user profiles.
  • Preference cookies that remember your settings and choices, such as language preferences or whether you have dismissed a notification banner.

You can manage your cookie preferences through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to block or delete cookies, though doing so may affect some features of our website. We do not use cookies for targeted advertising.

4

Data Storage and Security

We take the security of your personal information seriously. All data is stored on secure servers with industry-standard encryption protocols. We implement the following safeguards:

  • SSL/TLS encryption for all data transmitted between your browser and our servers.
  • Access controls that restrict personal data access to authorised editorial and technical staff only.
  • Regular security audits and vulnerability assessments of our infrastructure.
  • Secure backup procedures to prevent data loss.

While we strive to protect your information using commercially reasonable measures, no method of electronic storage or transmission over the internet is completely secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security but are committed to promptly addressing any breach that may occur.

5

Third-Party Services

We may use select third-party services to support the operation of our website. These services may have access to limited personal data as necessary to perform their functions. Our third-party integrations include:

  • Google Fonts for web typography delivery. Google may collect anonymised usage data in accordance with their privacy policy.
  • Hosting providers that store our website files and databases on secure infrastructure.
  • Email delivery services that facilitate our newsletter and communication systems.

We carefully vet all third-party services and ensure they comply with applicable data protection standards. We do not share your data with advertisers or data brokers.

6

Your Rights

You have the following rights regarding your personal information held by Mill-Times Mumbai:

  • Right to access: You may request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to correction: You may ask us to correct or update inaccurate or incomplete personal information.
  • Right to deletion: You may request the deletion of your personal data, subject to any legal obligations we may have to retain certain records.
  • Right to withdraw consent: Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw your consent at any time. This includes unsubscribing from our newsletter.
  • Right to data portability: You may request your data in a structured, commonly used, and machine-readable format.
  • Right to object: You may object to the processing of your personal data for specific purposes.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the details provided in the Contact Us section below. We will respond to your request within 30 days.

7

Children's Privacy

Mill-Times Mumbai does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. Our platform and services are intended for a general audience and are not directed at children. If we become aware that we have inadvertently collected personal data from a child under 13, we will take prompt steps to delete that information from our systems.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe that your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately so we can take appropriate action.

8

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, legal requirements, or operational needs. When we make changes, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page and, where appropriate, provide additional notice through our website or newsletter.

We encourage you to review this policy periodically to stay informed about how we are protecting your information. Your continued use of our website after any changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.

9

Contact Us

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please do not hesitate to get in touch:

  • Email: privacy@milltimes.in
  • Contact page: milltimes.in/contact
  • Address: Mill-Times Mumbai, Lower Parel, Mumbai 400013, Maharashtra, India

We are committed to resolving any privacy-related concerns and will respond to your inquiry as promptly as possible, typically within 30 days of receipt.
