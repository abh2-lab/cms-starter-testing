Who We Are

About Mill-Times

Community-powered journalism documenting the transformation of Mumbai's historic mill districts — one story at a time.

Our Story

Mill-Times Mumbai was born in 2024 from a simple observation: the most significant urban transformation in Mumbai's history was being documented by everyone except the people living through it. International media parachuted in for flashy features. Local outlets covered real estate deals. But the voices of mill workers, their families, the artists, small business owners, and long-time residents of Girangaon — they were missing from the record.

We set out to change that. Mill-Times is a platform built by and for the communities of Mumbai's mill belt — the stretch from Byculla to Worli that once powered the city's textile economy and now sits at the epicentre of India's most ambitious urban redevelopment.

Our Belief: The people closest to the story are best positioned to tell it. Every neighbourhood has a newsroom — it just needs a platform.

What We Cover

Our journalism focuses on the people, places, and policies shaping Mumbai's mill districts. We publish across several formats:

Investigations

Deep-dive reporting into land use, development approvals, heritage violations, and institutional accountability. Our investigations have led to public interest litigations and policy reviews.

Community Dispatches

First-person accounts and field reports from citizen journalists on the ground. These are the stories that don't make it to mainstream media — but matter most to the people living them.

Heritage Documentation

Archival photography, oral histories, and cultural mapping of the mill belt's rapidly disappearing industrial landscape. We work with historians, architects, and community elders to preserve what's being lost.

Photo Essays

Visual storytelling from the streets of Parel, Worli, Byculla, and beyond. Our photographers capture the textures of a neighbourhood in transition — from crumbling mill walls to gleaming glass towers.

Our Values

🎯Truth First

Every story is fact-checked and verified before publication. We don't trade accuracy for speed, and we correct mistakes openly.

🤝Community Owned

Our contributors are our neighbours. We report with the community, not just about it. Every byline carries the voice of the street.

🔓Radically Open

Our editorial process, funding sources, and correction policies are public. We believe transparency is the foundation of trust.

⚡Fiercely Independent

No political affiliations. No corporate sponsorship of editorial. Our newsroom answers to our readers — and no one else.
