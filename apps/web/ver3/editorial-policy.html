Our Standards

Editorial Policy & Ethics

Our commitment to truthful, community-driven journalism in Mumbai's mill districts.

Our Mission

Mill-Times Mumbai exists to amplify the voices of communities living in and around Mumbai's historic mill districts. We believe that the people closest to the story are best positioned to tell it. Our journalism is community-powered, editorially independent, and rooted in the lived experience of the neighbourhoods we serve.

From the textile workers of Girangaon to the artists reclaiming abandoned industrial spaces, our mission is to document the rapid transformation of these districts with honesty, nuance, and deep local knowledge. We serve readers who care about heritage, development, and the human stories behind urban change.

Core Principle: Every story we publish must serve the public interest of Mumbai's mill district communities. We do not publish for sensation; we publish for understanding.

Editorial Standards

Every piece of content published by Mill-Times Mumbai is held to four foundational standards. These are non-negotiable and apply equally to staff editors and community contributors.

Accuracy

We are committed to getting the facts right. All claims of fact must be supported by evidence. When information cannot be independently verified, we disclose that to our readers. We do not speculate, and we clearly distinguish between reporting, analysis, and opinion.

Fairness

We present all sides of a story. Individuals and organisations mentioned in our reporting are given a reasonable opportunity to respond before publication. We do not cherry-pick facts to support a predetermined narrative, and we treat all subjects of our reporting with dignity.

Independence

Mill-Times Mumbai operates independently of political parties, corporate interests, and government bodies. Our editorial decisions are made solely by our editorial team. We do not accept payments or incentives in exchange for coverage, and we clearly label all sponsored or partnership content.

Transparency

We are open about our methods, our sources (where safe to disclose), and our funding. We explain our editorial decisions when challenged, and we welcome public scrutiny of our work. Our corrections and clarifications are published prominently and promptly.

Editorial Independence: No advertiser, donor, or external party has influence over what we publish. Our editorial and commercial operations are strictly separated.

Verification Process

Every story submitted to Mill-Times Mumbai goes through a rigorous three-step verification process before publication. This ensures that our readers can trust the accuracy and integrity of everything we publish.

1

Submission Review

An editor reviews the initial submission for relevance, completeness, and adherence to our contributor guidelines. Stories are assessed for newsworthiness and public interest value.

2

Fact-Checking

Key claims, dates, names, and statistics are independently verified. Our fact-checking team cross-references sources, contacts relevant parties, and validates photographic and documentary evidence.

3

Editorial Approval

A senior editor conducts a final review for accuracy, fairness, legal compliance, and editorial quality. Only after this approval is the story cleared for publication.

Stories that do not pass any stage of this process are returned to the contributor with detailed feedback and guidance for revision. We would rather delay publication than compromise on accuracy.

Community Contributor Guidelines

Mill-Times Mumbai is built on the contributions of citizen journalists. We welcome submissions from anyone with a genuine story to tell about Mumbai's mill districts. Here is what we expect from our contributors:

  • Truthfulness: Only submit stories based on what you have personally witnessed, experienced, or can substantiate with evidence. Do not fabricate, embellish, or misrepresent events.
  • Original Work: All submissions must be your own original work. Do not submit content that has been plagiarised or copied from other sources without proper attribution.
  • Consent: If your story involves identifiable individuals, obtain their informed consent before submission. Respect the privacy of community members, especially vulnerable populations.
  • Context: Provide as much context as possible, including dates, locations, names of relevant parties (where appropriate), and any supporting media such as photographs or documents.
  • Disclosure: Inform us of any personal, financial, or political connection you may have to the subject of your story. This does not disqualify your submission, but transparency is essential for editorial assessment.
  • Respectful Tone: Write with respect for all individuals and communities involved. Avoid inflammatory language, unsubstantiated accusations, or content designed to harm rather than inform.
Your Voice Matters: You do not need a journalism degree or professional experience to contribute. What you need is a commitment to truth and a story that serves your community.

Corrections Policy

We take errors seriously. When we get something wrong, we correct it promptly, transparently, and proportionally. Our corrections policy applies to all published content, whether produced by staff or community contributors.

  • Minor Errors: Typographical errors, misspellings, and minor factual inaccuracies (such as incorrect dates or ages) are corrected directly in the text. A correction note is appended to the article with the date of the correction.
  • Significant Errors: Errors that materially affect the meaning or interpretation of a story are corrected with a prominent correction notice at the top of the article. The original error and the corrected information are both clearly stated.
  • Retractions: In cases where a story is found to be fundamentally flawed, unsupported by evidence, or fabricated, we retract the article entirely. A retraction notice is published in its place, explaining the reasons for the retraction.

If you believe a published story contains an error, please contact our editorial team at corrections@milltimes.in with specific details about the inaccuracy and any supporting evidence.

Conflict of Interest

All staff members and contributors to Mill-Times Mumbai are required to disclose any potential conflicts of interest that could compromise, or appear to compromise, the integrity of our reporting.

A conflict of interest may include, but is not limited to:

  • Financial interests in companies, developments, or organisations covered in a story
  • Personal relationships with individuals who are subjects of reporting
  • Political affiliations or activism related to the subject matter
  • Employment or consultancy arrangements with entities relevant to the coverage
  • Acceptance of gifts, hospitality, or other benefits from sources or subjects

Disclosed conflicts are assessed by the editorial team. In some cases, an alternative editor or contributor may be assigned to maintain objectivity. In all cases, relevant conflicts are disclosed to readers within the published piece.

Disclosure Over Disqualification: Having a connection to a story does not automatically prevent you from covering it. But failing to disclose that connection undermines the trust our readers place in us.

Source Protection

Mill-Times Mumbai is committed to protecting the identity and safety of our sources. Many of the stories we publish involve sensitive topics, including disputes with powerful developers, government accountability, and community grievances. We recognise that some contributors and sources face genuine risks by speaking out.

Our commitments regarding source protection:

  • Confidentiality: When a source requests anonymity, we honour that request. We do not reveal the identity of confidential sources to anyone outside the editorial team, including in response to legal demands, except as required by law.
  • Secure Communication: We provide secure channels for contributors who need to share sensitive information. We encourage the use of encrypted communication tools for high-risk submissions.
  • Minimised Exposure: We take care to remove identifying details from stories and media that could inadvertently reveal a source's identity. This includes metadata from photographs and documents.
  • Informed Consent: We ensure that all sources understand how their information will be used and what protections are in place before they share sensitive material with us.

If you wish to submit a story anonymously or with source protection, please indicate this clearly in your submission or contact us directly at secure@milltimes.in.

Complaints Procedure

We welcome feedback, criticism, and formal complaints about our journalism. If you believe that Mill-Times Mumbai has breached its editorial standards, you have the right to raise a complaint and receive a considered response.

How to File a Complaint

  1. Contact Us: Send your complaint to complaints@milltimes.in or use our contact form. Please include the headline or URL of the article in question, a description of the issue, and any supporting evidence.
  2. Acknowledgement: We will acknowledge your complaint within 3 working days.
  3. Investigation: The relevant editor will review the complaint, consult with the author and fact-checking team, and assess whether our editorial standards were breached.
  4. Resolution: We will provide a written response within 14 working days of acknowledgement. Where a breach is found, we will take appropriate action, which may include correction, clarification, apology, or retraction.
  5. Escalation: If you are not satisfied with the resolution, you may request a review by the Editor-in-Chief. This final review will be conducted within 21 working days.
Accountability: We do not view complaints as threats. They are an essential part of maintaining trust between Mill-Times Mumbai and the communities we serve. Every complaint helps us improve.
